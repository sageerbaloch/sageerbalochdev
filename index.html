About Sageer Baloch

My name is Sageer Baloch. I am from Balochistan, Pakistan. I am a student and I am curious to learn Cyber Security. I started from zero with no laptop, no mentor, only curiosity and a mobile phone. My dream is to become a Cyber Security Engineer and protect people, systems and data from hackers. I believe curiosity is the best teacher. Every day I am learning something new — one concept, one tool, one lab at a time. This website is my journey, my roadmap and my proof that a boy from a small city can become a big engineer.
